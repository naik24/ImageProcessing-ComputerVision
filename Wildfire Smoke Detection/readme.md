Wildfires are highly destructive, posing significant risks to both human life and the environment. They can devastate entire ecosystems, destroy homes and infrastructure, and lead to severe air quality issues that affect health. The rapid spread of wildfires, fueled by dry conditions and strong winds, makes them difficult to control once they have ignited. Early detection is crucial in mitigating these impacts, as it allows for quicker response times, enabling firefighters to contain the blaze before it escalates. This not only reduces the potential damage but also minimizes the resources needed for firefighting efforts and helps protect communities and natural habitats.


In this project, I have built a wildfire smoke detection system using `Roboflow` and `Ultralytics` to detect smoke in forests. This early detection can help in alerting the local authorities to take quick action.

## Implementation

I implemented two models:

1. YOLOv8
2. Roboflow Object Detection 3.0

## Metrics

1. YOLOv8
  - Precision: 0.869
  - Recall: 0.932
  - mAP: 0.956

2. Roboflow Object Detection 3.0
  - Precision: 0.97
  - Recall: 0.95
  - mAP: 0.92
